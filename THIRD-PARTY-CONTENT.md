# Third-party content

Vault Race bundles a few texts so that you can play without preparing anything. They are stored inside `main.js`; nothing is downloaded while you play.

The MIT License in [LICENSE](LICENSE) covers the program code of Vault Race and the texts written for it. It does **not** cover the third-party texts listed below; these keep their own terms.

## Wikipedia (track “Cities, countries, rivers”)

- Excerpts from 157 articles of the German Wikipedia and 157 articles of the English Wikipedia.
- Licence: [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
- Authors: the Wikipedia contributors of each article. The full list of articles with links to the exact versions is in [WIKIPEDIA-SOURCES.md](WIKIPEDIA-SOURCES.md). In the game, the source is shown below every note.
- Changes: shortened, cleaned up (pronunciation brackets, footnote markers), links set by Vault Race.
- If you reuse these excerpts, you must keep the attribution and the same licence.

## World English Bible (Bible track)

- Text: World English Bible, which is in the public domain. “World English Bible” is a trademark of eBible.org; the text is used unchanged.
- Source: https://ebible.org/web/

## OpenBible.info cross-references (Bible track)

- The links between Bible chapters come from the cross-reference data of [OpenBible.info](https://www.openbible.info/labs/cross-references/), licensed under [CC BY](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
- Change: for each verse only the best-rated reference with at least 5 votes is used.

## Written for Vault Race

- The 41 notes about fallacies and good arguing, in German and English.
- The key-fact sentences of the Europe track (borders, capitals, rivers, seas). The facts were checked against Wikipedia in October 2026.
